Poemline

Every text is a drawing waiting to happen.  |  How it works  |  About

Type any text and watch it become a unique geometric image. Each letter maps to a number, each word becomes a fraction, and fractions become coordinates for perpendicular strokes on a canvas.

The same text always produces the same image. Change a single letter and the whole picture shifts. A haiku and a novel both become a pattern of strokes — one sparse, one dense, both unrepeatable.
