Poemline

Text can be converted to numbers. Numbers can be drawn on a graph. This is a whimsical method of turning any text — past, present, or future — into a painting, one stroke per word.

The conversion is unique: no two texts will produce the same image. And each image could, in theory, be decoded back to the original text.*

Paste your favourite song, a poem, or anything you like — and see what it looks like on canvas.

* Subject to resolution constraints. For longer words you'd need atom-level precision to reverse it — but the maths checks out.
